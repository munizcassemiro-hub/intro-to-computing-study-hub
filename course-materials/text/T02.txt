Part 4 – Algorithm Design

Write an algorithm for making a cup of tea.

Your algorithm must:

Have a clear starting point.

Contain instructions in the correct order.

Include a decision about whether milk is required.

Have a clear ending.

Use pseudocode or numbered instructions.

For example:

START

Fill the kettle with water

...

IF milk is required THEN

    Add milk

END IF

...

STOP

Challenge

Swap your algorithm with another group.

The other group must follow your instructions exactly as written.

If they cannot complete the task, identify which instruction needs to be improved.

Part 5 – Debugging Challenge

Your teacher will provide an intentionally incorrect algorithm.

Example:

START

Pour water into the cup

Put the tea bag in the bin

Add milk

Boil the water

Put the tea bag in the cup

Drink the tea

STOP

Task

Identify at least 3 errors.

Rewrite the algorithm so that it works correctly.

Final Challenge – The Human Robot 

One student in each group becomes the “robot”.

The robot can only follow instructions given by the other students.

Give the robot instructions to:

Move from one side of the classroom to a specific object.

Rules:

The robot cannot make decisions for itself.

Instructions must be precise.

The robot can only move according to the instructions provided.

If an instruction is unclear, the robot must stop.
